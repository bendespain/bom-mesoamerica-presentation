# The Book of Mormon in Mesoamerica — One Page

## The claim
The lands and final wars of the Book of Mormon took place in the Maya lowlands: northern Guatemala (Petén), Belize, and southern Quintana Roo, Mexico — not in North America.

## The geography (Pasión River Model)
- **River Sidon** → the Pasión River, running north
- **Zarahemla** → Ceibal, Guatemala (Harvard excavations show six phases matching the Book of Mormon story)
- **Manti** → Cancuén · **Antionum** → Uxbenká · **Melek** → Aguateca · **Desolation** → Uaxactun
- **Narrow neck / pass** → the ~14-mile Tikal–Uaxactun corridor
- **Hill Cumorah** → Hill Achotal, northern Belize
- **Land of Nephi** → Salama Valley · **Hermounts** → Sierra del Lacandón jungle
- Shape: an upright hourglass — matching the text's relational map without rotation

## The four final wars (AD ~320–385)
1. **~327–350** — Lamanite war ends in the 350 treaty splitting the lands. Ceibal abandoned ~325. King Aaron ≈ Tikal's Foliated Jaguar. Gadianton robbers ≈ Caracol; their king dies ~349 and the city goes quiet.
2. **~360** — The Lamanite king's epistle to Mormon (Mormon 3:4) arrives the same year Great Jaguar Paw takes Tikal's throne (Stela 31).
3. **~375** — Assault on Desolation/Uaxactun. Extra manpower from Teotihuacan. The Tikal–Uaxactun earthwork matches the scriptural "fortification on the line."
4. **~379–385** — The **Entrada**: Teotihuacan's general Siyaj K'ahk' ("Fire Is Born") arrives AD 378; Mormon 5:5's 379th year follows one year later. Final stand at Cumorah ~385. 220,000 Nephite warriors; possibly 5 million Nephites in the land.

## Why it matters
23+ Maya monuments, stelae, murals, and a tomb tied to Mormon 1–6. Chronology, geography, and inscriptions converging into one cumulative case.

## Honest note
This is a proposed correlation (Terry & Roylance). Mainstream archaeology doesn't accept it. The argument is convergence — many independent lines pointing the same way — not any single proof.
