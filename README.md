# Book of Mormon in Mesoamerica — The Pasión River Model

A digestible, shareable presentation of Ben Despain's research correlating Book of Mormon geography and the final Nephite wars with ancient Maya sites in the Petén region of Guatemala and Belize.

## The 411 (short version)

**The claim:** The Book of Mormon lands sit in the Maya lowlands — northern Guatemala (Petén), Belize, and southern Mexico — not in North America.

**The geography:**
- **River Sidon** = the Pasión River, running north past Cancuén (Manti) to Ceibal (Zarahemla)
- **City of Zarahemla** = Ceibal, Guatemala — Harvard's Gordon Willey documented six occupational phases matching Zarahemla's history (Mulekite arrival, Mosiah influx, post-Christ peace, 350 AD abandonment)
- **Narrow neck / narrow pass** = the ~14-mile Tikal–Uaxactun corridor; Uaxactun = city of Desolation
- **Hill Cumorah** = Hill Achotal in northern Belize
- **Land of Nephi** = Salama Valley; **Hermounts** = Sierra del Lacandón jungle

**The wars (the newest material):**
- **~AD 327–350:** The war that produced the treaty. Ceibal abandoned ~325 AD. King Aaron of the Lamanites ≈ Foliated Jaguar of Tikal. Gadianton robbers ≈ Caracol (Te' K'ab Chaak), annihilated after the treaty.
- **~AD 360:** Great Jaguar Paw becomes king of Tikal the same year the Lamanite king sends an epistle to Mormon (Mormon 3:4).
- **~AD 375:** War begins at Uaxactun (Desolation). Extra manpower from Teotihuacan.
- **~AD 379–385:** The **Entrada** — Spearthrower Owl's general Siyaj K'ahk' ("Fire Is Born") arrives AD 378; Mormon 5:5's "379 years" follows one year later. Final stand at Hill Cumorah, ~385 AD. Estimated 220,000 Nephite warriors; possibly 5 million Nephites in the land.

**The cumulative case:** 23+ Maya monuments, stelae, murals, and a tomb matched to Mormon 1–6; chronology, geography, and inscriptions converging.
